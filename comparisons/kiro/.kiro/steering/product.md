# Product

Solar System viewer: a calm, good-looking way to watch the planets move and learn one fact
about each. Owner: sebastian.

## Goals
- Calm: it feels like a planetarium. Dark sky, soft glow, nothing jumps or overlaps.
- Learn: every body (the sun, 8 planets, the moon) can be clicked to show one true fun fact.
- No errors: the page runs without errors or warnings.

## Users
Anyone who opens the page, with a mouse or a touch screen.

## Out of scope
Elliptical orbits, true scale, sound, textures, moons other than Earth's, saving state.
